Deleting your Crux account

Crux is an AI resume checker. If you would like to delete your account and all associated data, follow the steps below.

How to request deletion

Email us at mrtension7@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account", sent from the email address registered to your Crux account.

What happens next

We permanently delete your account within 30 days of the request.

What data is deleted

  • Your account email and login credentials
  • All resume analyses and scoring history
  • Your usage quota records

What data is retained

None. All your data is removed once deletion is complete.
