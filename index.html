Deleting your Crux account

This page is for the Android app Crux: AI Resume Checker (developer: Qwark). If you would like to delete your account and the data linked to it, follow the steps below.

How to request deletion

Email us at abdulmoizofficial07@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account", sent from the Google account you use to sign in to Crux.

If you have a Crux Pro subscription, cancel it first in Google Play (Play Store, Payments & subscriptions, Subscriptions). Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription.

What happens next

We delete your account data within 30 days of your request and confirm by email.

What data is deleted

  • Your account details from Google Sign-In: name, email address, profile picture link and account ID
  • The names you gave your resumes in the app
  • Your ATS format check results
  • Cover letters you saved, and the job descriptions you pasted
  • Your free-action usage count
  • Your subscription records on our server (plan, status, expiry date and purchase token)

What is not deleted by this request

  • Usage statistics (Google Firebase Analytics), only if you allowed them. They are tied to a random app identifier, not to your account, so we cannot look them up by email. They are deleted automatically after 14 months. You can stop new collection any time in the app under Settings, Help improve Crux.
  • Content sent to Google's Gemini AI for analysis and your payment history in Google Play are held by Google under its own terms and retention rules.
  • Data on your phone (analysis history and settings): remove it in the app under Settings, Clear cache, or by uninstalling the app.

We do not keep a copy of the resume files you upload; they are processed and not stored on our servers.
